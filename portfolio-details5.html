Fraud Intelligence Pipeline

Credit Card Fraud Detection Technical Analysis Report

This report documents a complete end-to-end fraud intelligence pipeline built on 10,000 simulated credit card transactions. The objective was to identify high-risk behavioral segments, quantify fraud patterns, and deliver actionable business recommendations — using Python for preprocessing and EDA, PostgreSQL for structured analysis, and Power BI for interactive visualization.

The dataset is highly imbalanced — approximately 1.51% fraud — which reflects real-world credit card transaction distributions. Evaluation therefore prioritizes Recall, Precision, and ROC-AUC over raw accuracy.

The raw dataset was processed using Pandas prior to model training. Steps included checking for missing values/duplicates, statistical summaries, standardizing features using StandardScaler, and applying a Stratified 80/20 train-test split.

1. Foreign vs Domestic Risk

Foreign transactions exhibit a 4.08% fraud rate compared to 0.70% for domestic — representing a 3.2× higher risk, making it the single strongest individual risk signal.

2. High Velocity Impact

Customers with high transaction velocity (5+ transactions in 24 hours) represent only ~12% of total volume, yet account for ~38% of all fraud cases (4.75% fraud rate).

3. Device Trust Score

Low Trust devices (score 25–50) carry a 4.24% fraud rate (~4× baseline) and account for ~58.9% of all fraudulent cases.

4. Multi-Signal Combined Risk

When foreign origin and location mismatch occur together, the fraud rate spikes to 6.2%. Furthermore, combining low device trust, high velocity, and foreign origin creates a High Risk segment with an 8.75% fraud rate (13× baseline).

5. Merchant Categories & Time Windows

Travel (2.21%) and Grocery (2.06%) carry the highest fraud rates and total fraud amounts. Late-night transactions (0–5 AM) show a 2.81% fraud rate, which is 2.4× higher than daytime transactions.

  • Strengthen monitoring and cross-border verification for foreign transactions.
  • Implement dynamic rate-limiting for accounts exceeding 5+ transactions in 24 hours.
  • Trigger automated OTP/biometric re-verification for transactions with low device trust scores (<50).
  • Flag foreign + location mismatch combinations for immediate verification (6.2% risk).
  • Apply per-category fraud score thresholds targeting Travel and Grocery categories.
  • Deploy real-time Machine Learning risk scoring models into production pipelines.

  • Foreign origin, high velocity, and low device trust are the primary drivers of transaction fraud.
  • A multi-signal approach effectively isolates high-risk transactions with up to an 8.75% fraud rate.
  • Deploying all recommended controls offers a theoretical prevention potential of ~60% of detected fraud.
