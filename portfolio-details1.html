Marketing & Sales Performance Dashboard

This project analyzes 1,224 course registration transactions recorded over a one-year period from January to December to evaluate marketing and sales performance. The analysis covers key business dimensions, including monthly earnings and course registration trends, marketing platform contribution, learning program and skill-level performance, sales group performance, and location-based registration distribution. The findings were transformed into an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard that enables users to monitor key performance indicators, compare performance across different categories, identify business trends, and gain data-driven insights to support business decision-making.

Businesses need to monitor marketing and sales performance across multiple dimensions, including revenue, customer registrations, marketing channels, sales teams, products, and locations. However, when transactional data is presented primarily in raw tables, it can be difficult to quickly identify important business patterns, such as periods with the highest and lowest performance, the most effective revenue-contributing marketing platforms, high-performing learning programs, differences across sales groups, and areas with higher or lower registration activity. Without a centralized and interactive reporting system, monitoring these patterns can become time-consuming and make it more difficult to identify trends and areas for improvement. Therefore, this project transforms transactional data into an interactive dashboard that provides a comprehensive overview of marketing and sales performance.

  • Develop an interactive dashboard to centralize and visualize marketing and sales transaction data.
  • Analyze monthly earnings trends and course registration patterns.
  • Evaluate revenue contributions across different marketing platforms.
  • Analyze learning program and skill-level performance.
  • Compare revenue contributions across sales groups.
  • Analyze the distribution of course registrations across different area codes.
  • Provide data-driven insights to support business performance evaluation and decision-making.

The dataset consists of 1,224 course registration transactions recorded over a one-year period from January to December.

This project was developed using Microsoft Excel for data processing, analysis, and interactive dashboard development. Pivot Tables were used to aggregate and summarize transaction data, while Excel Charts visualized monthly trends and performance comparisons across key business dimensions. Slicers enabled interactive filtering by billing month and sales group.

The analysis applied KPI analysis, trend analysis, comparative analysis, and category-based performance analysis to transform transaction data into meaningful business insights.

1. Monthly Earnings Performance

Monthly earnings fluctuated significantly throughout the year. October recorded the highest earnings at ₹1,450,000, followed by November at ₹1,019,000, while March recorded the lowest earnings at ₹21,000.

2. Course Registration Trends

October recorded the highest number of registrations with 277 courses, while March recorded the lowest with 28 courses. The simultaneous peak in registrations and earnings during October indicates that registration volume was an important contributor to monthly revenue performance.

3. Marketing Platform Contribution

Television Ads generated the highest earnings at ₹1,845,000, followed by Facebook Page at ₹1,335,000 and WhatsApp at ₹1,040,000. Together, these three platforms contributed approximately 66.3% of total earnings.

Since marketing expenditure and conversion data were not available, these results represent revenue contribution rather than marketing ROI.

4. Learning Program Performance

BK was the dominant learning program, generating ₹4,553,000, or approximately 71.5% of total earnings. This indicates strong demand but also highlights a high concentration of revenue in a single product.

5. Skill Level Performance

KJI. L4 recorded the highest earnings contribution at ₹1,321,000, followed by Fdn. L5, Fdn. L1, and Pre. L3. The strong contribution from Foundation and Pre-level categories suggests that entry-level to intermediate learning segments represent important revenue sources.

6. Sales Group Performance

Jordan and Johnson were the highest-contributing sales groups, generating ₹2,154,000 and ₹2,034,000, respectively. Together, they contributed approximately 65.8% of total earnings.

7. Area Code Analysis

A101 recorded the highest number of course registrations with 216 registrations, followed by A201, B120, C800, and B180. These five areas collectively accounted for 75.7% of total registrations.

A401 recorded the lowest number of registrations with 96, suggesting an opportunity for further evaluation of regional performance.

  • October was the strongest month in terms of both revenue and course registrations.
  • March recorded the lowest performance in both earnings and registration volume.
  • Television Ad was the highest-contributing marketing platform based on total earnings.

  • KJI. L4 was the highest-contributing skill level.

  • Increase investment in high-performing marketing platforms, particularly Television Ads, Facebook Page, and WhatsApp, to maximize revenue contribution.
  • Improve sales training and performance strategies for lower-contributing groups, particularly Taylor and Morgan, by adopting best practices from Jordan and Johnson.
  • Launch targeted promotions and special campaigns during low-performing months, particularly March, to stimulate course registrations and revenue.
  • Promote high-revenue skill levels, particularly KJI. L4, Fdn. L5, Fdn. L1, and Pre. L3, while developing bundling and cross-selling opportunities.
  • Strengthen marketing efforts in high-performing areas, particularly A101, A201, B120, C800, and B180, while evaluating growth opportunities in lower-performing areas.
